Questions

How long does it take?

Most projects take 1 to 2 weeks once we have your content. We'll confirm the date before we start.

Who owns the domain?

You do. We register it in your name or transfer your existing domain to your account, and you keep control of it.

What happens if I want changes later?

Small edits are included in the monthly care plan. Without a plan, changes are billed at $75 per hour unless your quote says otherwise. You can also edit text and photos yourself.

Do I need to pay for email separately?

Yes. Professional email is a subscription from a provider such as Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, and it is billed to you directly. We set it up and make sure it is configured correctly.

Can you work with my existing site?

Often, yes. Send us the address and tell us what you want to change, and we'll tell you what is worth keeping.
