Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Small Biz Digital Solutions collects through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you use the quote form, we receive the name, email address, business name, and message you type in. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write.

How we use it

We use this information only to reply to your request, prepare a quote, and provide services you ask for. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who handles it

  • Formspree receives quote form submissions and forwards them to our email.
  • Google Workspace hosts our email.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and may log technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.

These providers have their own privacy policies. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this site.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as needed to respond and to keep business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email info@smallbizdigitalsolutions.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
